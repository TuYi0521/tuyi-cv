01About

I am a researcher working at the intersection of cryptography and machine learning. My PhD work at Nanyang Technological University focused on machine-learning-aided and SAT-aided cryptanalysis of symmetric-key primitives — developing new attacks and analysis tools for hash functions and block ciphers such as SHA-3, Keccak, AES-like hashing, and Simeck.

More recently my interests have expanded to AI safety, including safety evaluation of large language models, jailbreak attacks and defenses on text-to-image models, and bias analysis in video-language models.

02Research Interests

Cryptanalysis of Symmetric-Key Primitives
Differential / linear / algebraic attacks; SAT-based and quantum collision attacks on SHA-3 / Keccak; preimage and MITM attacks on AES-like hashing; conditional differential cryptanalysis.
Machine Learning for Cryptanalysis
Deep-learning-based differential distinguishers; neural cryptanalysis of lightweight block ciphers (Simeck); key-recovery attacks aided by neural networks.
AI Safety & Alignment
Safety evaluation with critique; jailbreak attacks and targeted defenses on text-to-image identity customization; positional bias in video-language models; preference tuning for faithful MLLMs.
